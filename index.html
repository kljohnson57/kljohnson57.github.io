Statistics in R Does Energy Make a Song Popular?

Spotify Energy vs. Popularity

Using almost 90,000 Spotify tracks from Kaggle, I tested whether higher-energy songs tend to be more popular. I fit a linear regression in R, checked the model assumptions, and found essentially no relationship (p = 0.72, R² close to 0).

The takeaway: how energetic a song sounds tells you almost nothing about how popular it will be, so things like artist, genre, and playlist placement probably matter much more.
